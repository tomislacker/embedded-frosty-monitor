## Context

What is the situation that forces a decision? What constraints, prior
decisions, or facts about the machines/hardware/firmware are driving this?

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in one place. Not a list of options
considered — the option chosen and why, briefly. Longer rationale belongs
in Context.

## Consequences

What this makes easier, what it makes harder, what it defers, and what it
commits us to. Include tradeoffs accepted knowingly, not just benefits.
