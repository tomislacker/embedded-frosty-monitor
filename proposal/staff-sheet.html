FrostSight

This box is monitoring this machine's health.

It does not change how the machine works.

YOUR JOB

If the machine does anything weird — noise, leak, wrong texture, warm drinks — press the button on the box.

That's it. You're done.

What counts as "weird"

  • A new or louder noise
  • Any leak, drip, or puddle
  • Drink texture off — too slushy or too hard
  • Drinks coming out warm
  • Machine seems to be working harder than usual
  • Anything else that just seems off
QR code here

Want to tell us more? Scan the QR code and add a note, photo, or video. It's optional — the button press alone is enough.

Do not unplug this box.
It runs on its own outlet and needs to stay recording the whole time it's here.
