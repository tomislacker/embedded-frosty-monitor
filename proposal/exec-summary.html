Executive Summary

Your frozen-drink machine is a revenue anchor, not just a piece of equipment — and when it fails, it fails expensively, on a schedule it chooses. Right now, diagnosing it is guesswork: a tech listens, looks, maybe catches the problem if it happens to act up during the one hour they're standing there.

What FrostSight does

We instrument your machine with 12+ sensor channels — motor and compressor electrical load, vibration at the drive and compressor, six temperature points through the refrigeration circuit, and control-system state — and record continuously, 24/7, for up to two weeks. At the end of that window you get a written diagnosis: what's actually wrong, and what it will take to fix it.

  • Fix the right thing the first time — no more parts-cannon repairs based on a hunch.
  • Repair-or-replace on data, not guesswork — know whether a $600–$1,700 compressor job is worth it against a $4,600–$5,300 new machine before you spend it.
  • Catch developing failures before they become a Friday-night dead machine.
See something, say something. Your staff press one button the moment something seems off — a noise, a leak, a bad pour. That moment is matched directly to the sensor record, so the report explains what actually happened, not just what the machine looked like on average.
