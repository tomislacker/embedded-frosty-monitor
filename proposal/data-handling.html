Data Handling Summary

This page is a plain-language summary of what FrostSight collects when monitoring your machine, what it doesn't, where it goes, and who owns it. It's meant to be read in under two minutes.

What we collect

Collected

  • Motor and compressor electrical load
  • Vibration at the beater drive and compressor
  • Temperatures at six points through the refrigeration circuit
  • Machine on/off and control-signal state
  • Timestamped journal-button presses
  • Optional: staff-submitted photos or videos, only if voluntarily added through the QR report form

Not collected

  • No audio
  • No video captured by us
  • No personal data about your staff or customers
  • No sales or point-of-sale data

Where it lives

Sensor data is recorded on the monitoring hardware during the deployment and encrypted at collection when it's pulled off at pickup. The optional staff report form (for the QR-code photo/video/note submissions) is cloud-hosted, so submissions from every shift land in one shared record instead of scattered across individual phones.

Who owns it

You own the data collected from your machine — you receive all of it as part of your report. FrostSight also keeps a copy to build and refine diagnostic baselines across machines; when used this way, it's anonymized beyond the site name.

Retention

We retain deployment data to maintain and improve our diagnostic reference. If you'd like your data deleted from our records after a report is delivered, contact us and we'll take care of it.
